About Me

Hello, my name is Yuntae Ju. My research focuses on epidemiology, statistical machine learning, and longitudinal data analysis, with a particular interest in understanding how lifestyle behaviors and physiological factors contribute to chronic disease risk, including obesity, metabolic syndrome, and cardiometabolic outcomes.

My academic background originates from quantitative analyses of human performance and physiological data, which gradually led me to broader questions in population health and chronic disease epidemiology. I am particularly interested in the statistical challenges associated with longitudinal, hierarchical, and observational datasets.

A central theme of my work is the integration of modern machine learning methods with rigorous epidemiologic and statistical principles. My research emphasizes robust validation, model interpretability, and generalizability in real-world health and sports science data.

My long-term goal is to develop statistically grounded machine learning frameworks for complex epidemiologic data, particularly in settings where prediction, interpretability, and valid inference must be considered simultaneously.

Representative Publications

Physical Activity Phenotypes Identified by Latent Class Analysis and Their Associations with Metabolic Syndrome Among Korean Adults

Ju, Y., Lee, J., Kim, D.-H., & Suh, S.-H.
Submitted to BMC Public Health
This study applied latent class analysis to nationally representative Korean adult data from the Korea National Health and Nutrition Examination Survey to identify multidimensional physical activity phenotypes. The study highlights the value of person-centered epidemiologic approaches for understanding behavioral heterogeneity and cardiometabolic disease prevention.

Artificial Intelligence-Driven Athlete Performance Passport for Enhancing Anti-Doping Strategies in Elite Male Weightlifting

Ju, Y., Lee, J., Kim, Y., Kim, D.-H., & Suh, S.-H.
Submitted to npj Digital Medicine
This study developed an artificial intelligence-driven Athlete Performance Passport framework using international weightlifting competition data. Longitudinal machine learning and explainable AI were integrated to support scalable and evidence-based anti-doping surveillance.

Performance Modeling for Men’s 100 m Freestyle: An Artificial Intelligence and Longitudinal Analysis Approach

Ju, Y., Oh, M., Lee, J., Kim, Y., Jang, S.J., Yoo, J., Park, K., Kim, Y., Ryoo, H., Kim, D.-H., & Suh, S.-H.
Submitted to Scientific Reports
This study combined artificial intelligence, explainable AI, and longitudinal mixed-effects modeling to investigate elite men's 100 m freestyle performance. The work demonstrates how predictive modeling and longitudinal statistical methods can advance data-driven performance evaluation.

Software

YTWGBS

YTWGBS is a graphical user interface software for whole-genome bisulfite sequencing analysis. It is designed to help researchers perform WGBS analysis without extensive command-line programming experience.

The software supports window generation, methylation data preprocessing, methylKit-based differential methylation analysis, DMR identification, visualization, and export-ready result tables.
