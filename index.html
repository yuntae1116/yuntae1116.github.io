About Me

I am a researcher trained in exercise physiology with a growing focus on physical activity epidemiology, cardiometabolic health, and healthy aging. I am interested in understanding why people with different patterns of movement and sedentary behavior experience different health and functional outcomes across adulthood.

My epidemiologic research uses population-based datasets and quantitative methods to characterize heterogeneity in physical activity and health. In my first-author study accepted by BMC Public Health, I identified distinct physical activity phenotypes using latent class analysis and evaluated their associations with metabolic syndrome among Korean adults.

Building on this work, I am particularly interested in device-measured physical activity, 24-hour movement patterns, mobility, and physical function as indicators of cardiometabolic risk and healthy aging. I hope to combine longitudinal cohort data, physiological measurements, and interpretable statistical methods to study how movement patterns relate to changes in health over time.

In parallel, I work as a bioinformatics researcher at Seoul National University, analyzing RNA-seq and whole-genome bisulfite sequencing data in a plant epigenetics setting. This work provides hands-on experience in genomic data processing, gene expression analysis, DNA methylation, and reproducible analytical software development.

My long-term goal is to integrate behavioral and wearable-derived phenotypes with physiological and molecular biomarkers—including metabolomic and other omics measures—to investigate biological heterogeneity in cardiometabolic health and aging. I view machine learning and computational biology as complementary tools for asking meaningful epidemiologic questions, not as ends in themselves.

Research Interests

Questions and methods connecting movement, physiology, disease risk, and aging.

01 · MOVEMENT

Physical Activity & Wearable Phenotyping

Multidimensional activity patterns, sedentary behavior, accelerometry, and 24-hour movement profiles. I am interested in characterizing person-level differences beyond average activity volume.

02 · AGING

Cardiometabolic Health & Physical Function

Associations of movement and behavioral phenotypes with metabolic risk, cardiovascular health, mobility, and functional aging, with particular interest in longitudinal change.

03 · METHODS

Epidemiologic & Computational Methods

Latent class analysis, survey-weighted regression, longitudinal modeling, interpretable machine learning, and emerging interests in causal inference and bias-aware observational research.

04 · BIOLOGY

Biomarkers, Omics & Bioinformatics

Hands-on RNA-seq and DNA methylation analysis, with a longer-term interest in linking behavioral exposures to physiological and molecular phenotypes of aging and disease.

Long-term research direction

Can wearable-derived movement patterns, physiological measurements, and molecular biomarkers jointly identify early changes in functional and cardiometabolic health? How can these signals improve our understanding of heterogeneity in healthy aging?

Selected Publications & Manuscripts

Selected first-author research relevant to epidemiology and quantitative methods.

Accepted · In production

Physical Activity Phenotypes Identified by Latent Class Analysis and Their Associations with Metabolic Syndrome Among Korean Adults

Ju, Y., Lee, J., Kim, D.-H., & Suh, S.-H. (2026). BMC Public Health.
DOI: 10.1186/s12889-026-29957-8

Identified four physical activity phenotypes using latent class analysis in a study of 15,370 Korean adults, examined class reproducibility, and estimated associations with metabolic syndrome using survey-weighted models.

Manuscript · Under review

Artificial Intelligence-Driven Athlete Performance Passport to Enhance Anti-Doping Strategies in Elite Male Weightlifting

Ju, Y., Kim, D.-H., & Suh, S.-H. Scientific Reports.

Applied longitudinal modeling and machine learning to international competition records to characterize individual performance trajectories and support an explainable, data-driven anti-doping research framework.
